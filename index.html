About CalcPlus

CalcPlus is a small independent project built to make everyday math painless. Every tool on this site runs instantly in your browser: nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and nothing is sold. We keep the interface light so the pages load fast even on slow mobile connections.

Each calculator was coded and checked by hand. If you spot a wrong result, please email us — user reports are how this site gets better. All tools are free forever, with no sign-up and no limits.
